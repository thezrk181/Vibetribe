Zaryab Khan

I am a creative based in the city, obsessed with visual storytelling. Photography, to me, is not just about capturing light—it’s about freezing a feeling.


Whether it’s the symmetry of urban architecture or the chaotic beauty of nature, I look for the stories that others walk past. This portfolio is a collection of my journey, a visual diary of the world as I see it.
